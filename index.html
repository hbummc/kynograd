Where we are

Phase 1: the guild quarter

Phase 0 laid the foundations: the Lykeion, the town square and the People's Bank. In Phase 1 the Police HQ goes up and every guild leads a project, and the guild quarter grows around a central arena with one hall per guild. Every gold donation helps the town grow so more comrades can join the cause.

The town now

Kynograd sits where the north river meets the east river. The town square is in the corner where they meet, and both bridges start there: north to the farm, east to the tree farm.

      Join a guild

      1. Pick a guild from the roster above.
      2. Join the Kynograd Discord with the button below and accept the rules in #rules.
      3. Say you're interested in the guild's channel: your EarthMC name and what you're good at.
      4. Earn the head's trust by helping with your guild's project. The head gives the rank.
